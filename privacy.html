Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Pet Health Diary does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Data on your device

Your pets' details, care schedule and weight log is saved only on your iPhone. It never leaves your device unless you choose to share it yourself, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your phone. No data is sent to a server.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
